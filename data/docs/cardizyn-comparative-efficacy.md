---
product: Cardizyn
title: Cardizyn - Comparative Efficacy
---

In the head-to-head programme Cardizyn produced a mean reduction in systolic
blood pressure comparable to the standard second-line comparator, with fewer
discontinuations for peripheral oedema.

Cardizyn and Nexovar are not alternatives to one another. Cardizyn treats
hypertension; Nexovar treats heart failure with reduced ejection fraction. They
appear together in cardiology conversations because the same clinicians
prescribe both, not because they compete.

No trial has evaluated Cardizyn and Nexovar in combination. Where a patient is
on both, the clinical rationale is the two separate indications.
