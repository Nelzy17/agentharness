Nexovar dosing questions dominate cardiology conversations this quarter. The
pattern across accounts is consistent: clinicians accept the efficacy data and
then stall on the practical dosing detail, almost always around renal function.

Three questions come up repeatedly. What is the starting dose when eGFR is
between 30 and 60. Whether the titration interval changes. Whether potassium
monitoring differs from the general population. All three are answered in the
dosing and administration document, and the field team should route to that
rather than answering from memory.

A secondary theme is missed doses in the elderly. There is no dose banking with
Nexovar and the guidance is simply to resume the next scheduled dose.
