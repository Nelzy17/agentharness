---
product: Nexovar
title: Nexovar - Dosing and Administration
---

Nexovar is initiated at 25 mg once daily with food. Dosing is reviewed after two
weeks and, where blood pressure and potassium allow, doubled to the 50 mg
maintenance dose. The maximum licensed dose is 100 mg once daily.

## Renal impairment

In moderate renal impairment (eGFR 30-59 mL/min/1.73m2) the starting dose is
halved to 12.5 mg once daily and the titration interval is extended from two
weeks to four. Potassium and creatinine should be checked before each dose
increase. In severe renal impairment (eGFR below 30) Nexovar is not
recommended, as the pivotal programme excluded these patients.

## Hepatic impairment

No dose adjustment is required in mild hepatic impairment. Nexovar has not been
studied in moderate or severe hepatic impairment.

## Missed doses

A missed dose may be taken the same day. Two doses should never be taken
together to make up a missed dose.
