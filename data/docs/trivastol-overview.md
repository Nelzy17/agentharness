---
product: Trivastol
title: Trivastol - Overview
---

Trivastol is an adjunct to metformin in type 2 diabetes where glycaemic control
remains inadequate. Dosing is 5 mg once daily, taken in the morning, with no
titration step.

When Trivastol is added to a sulfonylurea the risk of hypoglycaemia rises and a
reduction in the sulfonylurea dose should be considered. No reduction is needed
alongside metformin alone.

Trivastol is weight neutral over 52 weeks, which is the point endocrinologists
most often raise in comparison to the older agents.
