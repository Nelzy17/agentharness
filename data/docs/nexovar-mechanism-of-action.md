---
product: Nexovar
title: Nexovar - Mechanism of Action
---

Nexovar is a selective cardiac myosin modulator. It binds the myosin heavy chain
in a state that lengthens the duration of systolic ejection without increasing
intracellular calcium, which is the mechanism that distinguishes it from the
older inotropes and explains its arrhythmia profile.

The result at the level of the ventricle is a measurable increase in stroke
volume at an unchanged heart rate. In the pivotal programme this translated into
a reduction in the composite endpoint of cardiovascular death and heart failure
hospitalisation over 24 months.

Because the effect is load-dependent, the clinical response to Nexovar is
larger in patients with the lowest baseline ejection fractions. This is the
subgroup finding cardiologists most often ask to see.
