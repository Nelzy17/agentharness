---
product: Nexovar
title: Nexovar - Safety Profile
---

The most frequently reported adverse events with Nexovar are hyperkalaemia,
dizziness and a transient rise in serum creatinine within the first month.

Hyperkalaemia was the most common reason for discontinuation in the pivotal
trial. Potassium should be measured at baseline, before each dose increase
during titration, and every three months on maintenance dosing.

Nexovar is contraindicated in pregnancy and in patients with a history of
angioedema. Concomitant use with potassium-sparing diuretics requires closer
monitoring rather than avoidance.
