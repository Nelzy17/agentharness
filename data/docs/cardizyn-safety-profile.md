---
product: Cardizyn
title: Cardizyn - Safety Profile
---

Peripheral oedema, headache and flushing are the adverse events most often
reported with Cardizyn. Oedema is dose-related and usually appears within the
first eight weeks.

Cardizyn is contraindicated in severe aortic stenosis. Grapefruit juice raises
plasma concentrations and should be avoided.

Routine laboratory monitoring is not required beyond standard hypertension
follow-up.
