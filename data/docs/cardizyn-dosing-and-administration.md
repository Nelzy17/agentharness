---
product: Cardizyn
title: Cardizyn - Dosing and Administration
---

Cardizyn is a second-line antihypertensive dosed at 10 mg once daily, titrated
in 10 mg increments at four-week intervals to a maximum of 40 mg once daily.

The four-week titration interval reflects the time to steady state and is the
most common source of clinic friction, since it lengthens the time to a settled
dose for patients seen monthly.

No dose adjustment is required in mild to moderate renal impairment. Cardizyn
may be taken with or without food.
